3. If there is repeated non-atomic modulation with recurring handed or mirror-symmetry-breaking motifs in the topograph or dI/dV map, classify as chiral CDW.

4. If the evidence is weak but visibly consistent with CDW, prefer CDW over non-CDW.

5. If the evidence is weak but visibly consistent with repeated handed CDW motifs, prefer chiral CDW over CDW.

6. Do not infer chirality from a single isolated defect, random noise, scan distortion, or one non-repeated asymmetric feature.
